ByteSized AI Uploader – Privacy Policy

ByteSized AI Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the ByteSized AI YouTube channel to upload their own videos and thumbnails via the YouTube Data API.

It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other users. OAuth tokens are stored privately by the owner and are used solely to upload content to the owner's channel.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: via the ByteSized AI YouTube channel.
